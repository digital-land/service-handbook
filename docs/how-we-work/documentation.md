---
title: Documentation
---

Here's a few simple guidelines to follow around documentation. Though the [Agile Manifesto](https://agilemanifesto.org/) emphasises working software over comprehensive documentation, and individuals and interactions over processes and tools, there are times when we do need to document software, processes and tools. 

When we're working on something new, like a brand new piece of software or process, it's likely to change often. It'll also need optimising over time. Early on it's best to document key decision points, for example, by using a design history or writing architecture decision records. It helps people understand how something came into being.

When software or processes are more established, good documentation helps other people use the software or processes.
